Here is a clear overview of recent cybersecurity breaches, focusing on their main causes and impacts.

1.  Ransomware Attack (2024)
Cause:

A ransomware group exploited stolen login credentials and the absence of multi-factor authentication (MFA) on a remote access system.

Impact:

Major disruption to healthcare billing and pharmacy services across the United States.

Sensitive patient and insurance data potentially exposed.

Hospitals and pharmacies struggled to process claims for weeks.

2.  Data Breach Campaign (2024)

Common Causes of Recent Cybersecurity Breaches
Across many incidents, several patterns appear:

1. Weak Authentication
Lack of multi-factor authentication (MFA)

Stolen or reused passwords

2. Phishing and Social Engineering
Employees tricked into revealing credentials

Fake emails and login pages

3. Vulnerable Software
Unpatched systems

Exploitable security flaws

4. Third-Party Risks
Vendors or cloud platforms compromised

5. Malware and Ransomware
Systems encrypted and data stolen for ransom

Major Impacts of Cybersecurity Breaches
1. Financial Loss
Ransom payments

System recovery costs

Regulatory fines

2. Data Exposure
Personal, financial, or medical records leaked

3. Operational Disruption
Services shut down or slowed for days or weeks

4. Reputational Damage
Loss of customer trust

Decline in market value

5. Legal Consequences
Lawsuits and government investigations
